How to play

  1. Tap Play and enter at least two first names (up to eight).
  2. Keep “All themes”, or pick a few. Choose 30, 45 or 60 seconds for each set of four questions.
  3. Pass the phone to the asker. They press and hold “Let’s go” once they hold the device. The person answering must not look at the screen.
  4. The asker reads out loud and taps Right or Wrong. After four questions, each miss draws a stamp: close call or hitch. Three hitches and you stop answering, but you still ask.
  5. Every right answer lengthens the trip. The last copilot still in the relay reaches the finish; the kilometers are the family’s souvenir.

No account

There is no sign-in. Past games stay on this device only. Leaving a game from Menu does not save it.

Questions repeat

The pack is finite (2,000+ questions). The app sets recently played questions aside from one game to the next. Turn on more themes if the table recognizes too many questions.

Timer

The timer covers all four questions of the turn, not each one. When time runs out, the remaining questions are skipped and count as misses for the stamps.

Language

FamiliQuiz follows your device language: English or French.
