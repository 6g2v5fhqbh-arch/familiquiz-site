FamiliQUIZ

Privacy policy · Effective August 21, 2026

FamiliQuiz is the quiz you pass from hand to hand. It works offline. It does not create an account and does not send your data to our servers — we don’t have any.

Data on this device

The app may store, locally only:

  • The first names entered for a game
  • Theme and timer preferences
  • The IDs of recently played questions (to limit repeats)
  • Up to 50 finished games (finish, kilometers, players, themes, date)

This uses Apple’s UserDefaults on the device. Deleting the app erases this data. We cannot read it.

What we don’t collect

No email, phone number, Apple ID, location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising identifier, analytics, or crash reports sent to us. No tracking. No third-party advertising or analytics kits.

Internet

Playing requires no network. The question pack ships inside the app. Apple may still use the network for App Store receipts and system services; that is Apple’s processing, not ours.

Children

The game is made for families. It collects no personal data off the device and does not build profiles of children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also appear in the App Store privacy section when required.
